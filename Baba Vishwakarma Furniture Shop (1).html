Our Story

Craftsmanship You Can Feel

Founded in 1985 in Patna, Bihar, Baba Vishwakarma Furniture Shop was built on a single belief: that beautiful furniture should be accessible to every Indian family. What began as a small workshop has grown into one of the most trusted furniture names across Bihar and Jharkhand.

Every piece we craft carries the legacy of three generations of master woodworkers. We work exclusively with kiln-dried teak, solid sheesham, and select engineered hardwoods — materials chosen for their beauty, strength, and longevity. Our joinery methods blend time-honoured mortise-and-tenon techniques with modern CNC precision.

Today, with over 50,000 satisfied families, we continue to put quality and comfort at the heart of everything we make.
